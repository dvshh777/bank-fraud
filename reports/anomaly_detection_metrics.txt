5. KEY OBSERVATIONS & ARCHITECTURAL INSIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Unsupervised Signal vs Supervised Discriminator:
    - IsolationForest achieves ROC-AUC of 0.9379, demonstrating strong general outlier separation,
      but achieves low PR-AUC (0.1534) due to high false-alarm rates among extreme legitimate outliers.
  * Nature of XGBoost False Negatives:
    - Frauds caught by XGBoost (TPs) have a high mean anomaly score (+0.1087),
      whereas missed frauds (FNs) have a negative mean anomaly score (-0.0472),
      confirming that missed frauds mimic normal transaction topology in PCA space.
  * Complementary Recovery Potential:
    - The top 5% most anomalous transactions (9,969 tx) recover 18 of the 65 missed frauds (27.69%),
      highlighting a viable candidate signal for hybrid tier-based review routing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POLICY & TRADEOFF ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * No single threshold is universally optimal.
  * In fraud detection, the threshold represents an explicit business tradeoff between:
    1. False Negatives (Frauds missed -> direct financial loss for bank/merchant)
    2. False Positives (Legitimate transactions flagged -> manual review costs, customer friction)
  * For instance, setting threshold=0.99 yields highest F1 (0.6690) and 56.4% precision with only
    502 flagged transactions, but allows 61 frauds to pass undetected.
  * Conversely, setting threshold=0.20 captures 95% of all frauds (327 caught), but flags 18,524 transactions
    (9.29% of all traffic), requiring high operational review bandwidth.
